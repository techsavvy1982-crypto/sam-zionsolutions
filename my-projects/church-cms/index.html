People

Every family, remembered.

Member and family IDs, photo, address, zone, work, denomination, baptism and confirmation, fellowships — one form that the rest of the system reads.

The office can actually use it.

Search, Excel export, bulk print, family records, and a restore path for deleted members. 883 active members on the live St. Paul’s dashboard.

  • Personal + church details on one member form
  • Dashboard: families, gender, age bands, ministry participation
  • Feeds receipts, wishes, statements, and reports
Automatic wishes

The card goes out.
The office didn’t type it.

Birthdays and anniversaries from the roll

Upcoming seven days on one board. Send a WhatsApp greeting with a generated card and a Bible verse — English and Tamil in the verse library.

Turn on the clock

Auto greetings at a set time. Weekly report to office bearers on the day you choose. Manual send still available when you want to review first.

You stay in control

Skip list for members who should not be wished. Card templates, send log, PDF and Excel reports for any date range.

  • WhatsApp card + verse, church name on the image
  • Auto or one-tap send
  • Weekly digest for treasurer / secretary
  • Exclusions and full history
Phone directory

Everyone except
the congregation roll.

People and organisations

Vendors, diocese, government, school, hospital, bank — contacts the office actually calls. This is not the member database.

Your categories, your tree

Nested categories you drag into place. Search, A–Z, phone, WhatsApp, email, notes. Excel export when the committee asks for a list.

  • Separate from members on purpose
  • Role-gated; master password on sensitive actions
  • Changes land in the audit trail
Asset management

Paper that used to live in a cupboard.

Invoices, warranty cards, contracts — stored, categorised, and watched. The Documents vault alerts before expiry (renew in 3 days, 10-day warning).

Movable inventory

One line per item, photo, location and type trees, condition, stock in / out / return.

Fixed property

Buildings and complex records, with documents attached. Tabs can be granted separately so not every login sees land papers.

  • Active and archive (warranty ended)
  • Category tree: invoices, warranty, receipts, certificates, contracts
  • Preview the scan beside the record
Events & registers

The register is not the calendar.

Event Recorder keeps Baptism, Confirmation, Wedding, and Burial — serial numbers (0002/2026), dates, both parties, documents, bulk export.

Planner for programmes

Year, month, week, agenda, and Kanban. Recurring events, tasks, volunteers, WhatsApp, Excel. Different job from the sacramental books.

  • Four registers in one module
  • Search by serial number
  • Colour-coded bride and groom columns on weddings
Sunday finance

The receipt is the Sunday moment.

Numbered receipt (FY 2026-27_002768), member lookup, months paid as a grid, cash / cheque / DD / net banking / UPI, category lines, WhatsApp PDF to the member.

Before and after the counter

Declarations for the year. Payment schedule (monthly to annual) and a public UPI pay link so the member can settle without logging in.

Reports the PC meeting needs

Member report, member statement, receipt report, auction with year-close, transfer report.

  • Categories: subscription, fellowships, FBRF, village ministry, diocesan funds…
  • Always in the project — this is not the Accounts add-on
Accounts — optional

Keep Tally, or keep books
in the CMS.

Accounts are included when the church opts in — no extra price. Leave them off and continue in Tally. Receipts and members still run in the CMS.

Stay on Tally

CMS for people and receipts. Books stay where your auditor already works.

Simple Accounts

Cash book in plain language. Money in, money out, categories, basic reports. For treasurers who are not accountants.

Advanced Accounts

Lock Single entry or Double entry. Chart of accounts, vouchers, trial balance, statements, budget vs actual. Multiple books — church, shopping complex, trust, school.
